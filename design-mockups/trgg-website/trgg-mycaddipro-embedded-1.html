Our direction

Better golf, less paperwork.

Travellers Rest Golf Group has always run on the goodwill of a few people with a clipboard. As we've grown, that's got harder — draws take longer to publish, results take longer to confirm, and handicaps depend on someone finding the time to update them.

Moving the society onto MyCaddiPro is our answer to that. It commits us to three things:

  • Fair competitionHandicaps maintained properly and consistently, so every division is worth winning.
  • Nothing hiddenFixtures, draws, results and standings visible to every member — not just whoever was there when they were announced.
  • More golf, less adminOur volunteers spend their time organising golf, not adding up cards.

What isn't changing: the courses we play, the people we play with, and the welcome for anyone who turns up. The golf stays exactly as it is — we're just looking after the details better.

Getting set up · Three steps

Two minutes, once.

Do this at home with a cup of tea, not on the first tee.

01

Open it in your phone's browser

Go to mycaddipro.com. Nothing to find in the App Store or Play Store.

02

Add it to your home screen

Puts an icon on your phone so it opens like any other app.

iPhone

Safari → Share button → Add to Home Screen.

Android

Chrome → three dots → Install app.

03

Sign in and register

Tap Sign in with LINE, then fill in your details. Enter your name exactly as TRGG has it on the fixture list — that's how your handicap and past results find you.

Played with us before?

Your handicap and results are already in there — registering just connects them to your login. If anything looks wrong, tell a committee member.

Visiting Pattaya

Not a member? Come and play.

Visitors are welcome at our events. Set it up the same way, check the fixture list, and put your name down as a guest — it's the quickest way to see where we're playing next.
