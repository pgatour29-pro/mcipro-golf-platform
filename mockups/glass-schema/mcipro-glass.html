Screen 02 · Society Events

The card didn't change. The sheet under it went away.

Same .scv3g-ec: date cube left, society logo + course name, the mono meta line, the tag row, the fill bar, and the one-button-wide action column with fee → REGISTER → +GUESTS → mini icons. Same border states — green for today, blue for you're-in, red for cancelled.

What the glass buys is separation: on a flat grey sheet the cards all read as one block, and the tag row has to shout to be found. Over a photographic ground each card is a pane with light behind it, so the border states carry the whole message and the tags can sit quiet.

Screen 03 · Live Scoring · TRADV

The one screen that has to survive the sun.

Every control is where it is now: ‹ HOLE chip ›, F9/B9, the keypad swap, the red END; the card with its par row, birdie circles and bogey squares, the green selected cell, the per-player totals; the entry panel with the six-key window centred on net par; Leaderboard / Games / Finish Round.

The glass is deliberately thinner here — 5.5% white instead of 7.5%, and the card sits on a heavily blurred ground. The scorecard is a reading surface first. And the sunlight rule still governs: on a bright fairway SUN flips this whole view to the flat high-mass palette, because a photographic ground is the first thing 40,000 lux destroys.

Screen 01 · Overview

Eight cubes, same order, same art.

Tee Time, Caddy, Food & Orders, Society Events, Play Golf, History, Messages, Event Results — order 1 through 8, four across, exactly as the phone renders them today. The CPV1 poster illustrations are the real SVG sprite, not redrawn. Play Golf stays the ▶ triangle, white-filled with the green stroke and your avatar in it.

The one change worth arguing about: the poster tiles' pastel washes were opaque sheets. Here each cube keeps its own hue as a 13–16% tint over the glass, so the tiles are still individually identifiable at a glance but the ground reads through them. Everything else the mobile CSS already does — hiding descriptions, hiding pills except .cube-live, shrinking the Coming Soon badge — is untouched.

Two things I had to correct rather than copy: the Coming Soon badge was red-on-near-white, which is a bright sticker on a dark pane, so it becomes a red-on-tinted-glass chip; and the .cube-live pills were light-mode Tailwind fills (bg-orange-50 etc.) that would have been white blocks. They keep their colours as translucent chips.
