Golf in Pattaya, run from your phone

MyCaddiPro is the golf platform for Pattaya and the eastern seaboard: society registration, live tee sheets, caddy booking by number, live scoring, handicaps and results, on your phone.

What it does

Society daysRegister for a day in two taps, see who is playing, transport and fees. Organizers get registrations, pairings and a tee sheet that updates live.
Caddies by numberRequest the caddy you want when you register. Pro shops run their caddy desk and tee sheet on the same data.
Live scoringA paper-card view that fits one screen: scores, Stableford points, side games, leaderboards and results the moment the card is turned in.
Handicaps and resultsSociety and universal handicaps kept from scored rounds; results, order of merit and player of the year.
Pro shop and course staffLive tee sheet, day view, quick find, caddy desk and a permanent venue QR for walk-in guests.
Four languagesEnglish, Thai, Korean and Japanese throughout the app.

Courses

The societies play 24 courses around Pattaya, Chonburi and Rayong. Every course has its own page with the scorecard, yardage, who plays there and when, and a venue link for visitors.
