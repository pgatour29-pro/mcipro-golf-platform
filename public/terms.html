1. Who can use MyCaddiPro

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a binding agreement. Course staff, caddie master and organizer roles are granted by the course or society concerned; you may not claim a role you are not entitled to.

3. What the service is

MyCaddiPro provides live scoring, handicaps, leaderboards, society event registration and results, tee-time and caddy bookings with participating courses, course offers, messaging between golfers, societies, caddies and courses, Tap-In (a photo and short-video feed for golf) and the 19th Hole (a noticeboard where members list golf gear and services).

4. Bookings, fees and cancellations

  • Tee times and caddies are booked with the golf course. The course's own rules on prices, deposits, arrival and no-shows apply.
  • Society event fees are set and collected by the society organizer, not by MyCaddiPro. Amounts shown in the app are the organizer's record.
  • MyCaddiPro does not process card payments and does not charge subscription fees.
  • You can cancel a tee time or a caddy booking you made from the app; the course and society can also cancel or change bookings. A caddy cannot cancel a booking herself; changes are made by the course.
  • Hot deals are limited offers made by a course; spots are first come, first served and a grab is a booking with the course.

5. Scores, handicaps and results

Scores you or your playing partners enter are shared with your group, your society and its organizers, and on leaderboards you take part in. Organizers may correct scores and publish results. Handicaps shown in the app are calculated from posted rounds and society records and are for the app's own competitions unless your society states otherwise. A "Verified round" on Tap-In is read from the round recorded in the app; it cannot be typed in.
